About DentaCare

Our story, mission, and the team behind your smile.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

DentaCare Clinic was founded in 2017 with a single vision: to provide world-class dental care that is accessible, comfortable, and patient-centred. Located in the heart of Gaborone, we have grown into one of Botswana's most trusted dental practices, serving thousands of patients each year.

We combine the latest dental technology with a compassionate approach, ensuring every patient leaves with a healthier, more confident smile. From routine cleanings to complex restorative procedures, our team is equipped to handle all your dental needs under one roof.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional dental care through evidence-based practice, patient education, and a warm, welcoming environment — empowering every patient to achieve and maintain optimal oral health.
